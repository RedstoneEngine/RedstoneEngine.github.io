A Little About Me!

    Hello! My name is Gavin and I am a passionate Creative Engineer! I love the intersection of all things STEM and the Arts; Solving complex math problems while providing as much jam-packed entertainment to others as possible!

    I grew up in the Texas Coastal Bend Area and graduated in May 2025 from the University of Texas at Austin as a Mechanical Engineering Major with a minor in Computer Science and Entrepreneurship. I choose this for the love of the Knowledge!

    I absolutely love diving deep into unknown worlds, designing immersion around living ecosystems, planning interesting experiences for others, and sitting down at the end of the day to listen to the breakdown of a complex algorithm! I hope this website serves as a useful catalogue of the projects I've been working on as well as a source of learning and inspiration for your own journey!
