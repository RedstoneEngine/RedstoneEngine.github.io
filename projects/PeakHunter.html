Peak Hunter


    Peak Hunter is a unique spin on a recent game that I've loved playing, PEAK. In PEAK your goal is to climb your way past many climates and obstacles as you attempt to reach the Peak and leave on a helicopter. In my mod Peak Hunter before starting, you select your roles: Climber or Hunter. Climbers have the same goal, reach the top, except 1 random climber will receive a stun blowgun on a cooldown to be used against the Hunter(s). Hunters, however, must stop the Climbers from reaching the Peak using a new explosive hand attack!

    This was my first plugin mod where I had to use patching to modify the behavior of native Unity game functions, and while challenging was worth it as it gained over 8k downloads in 4 months! This mod was made primarly as a unique video idea for a new collaborative channel BloopTank and the video was also a success reaching 25k views and an additional 80 subscribers in 1 month from an almost empty channel! I have since due to community requests, added a few more features, improved balancing, and fixed bugs!
