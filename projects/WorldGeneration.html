World Generation


    World Generation has become a long and fun journey of mine these past few months. From learning everything I can about Minecraft's World Generation to learning how to use the recent game Hytale's world generation tools to then providing many guides and creating many of my own worlds utilizing all that I have learned!

    What started out as a hobby has become so much more and has easily become one of my top job prospects combining my love of math with my joy of creating art, experiences, and ecosystems! I hope to implement this knowledge into my Unity and Unreal projects designing more and more game pieces that blend into it such as the player interactions, the story, and creatures that reside in it. I have listed below my many different guides, worlds, and pieces of art, but as I have done a lot on Twitter and Discord, I'm always open to answer any questions and help out with others with their world building projects!

The Prehistoric Journey


    Since starting my World Gen journey, I have created 2 whole worlds: Wormhole Ascent which is featured as a different project and The Prehistoric Journey! The Prehistoric Journey was a week long world gen contest with the theme Raptor Hollows. My submission is linked here!

    In this mod, I developed a world with 2 biomes: The Jungle and Cursed Desert. The Jungle came out exactly how I envisioned it with odd yet navigable terrain, interesting Points of Interest, and a bit of a story. My favorite parts being traversing the dirt tunnels and viewing the place at night with all the different colored lighting! My least favorite part I'd want to continue working on is the desert. There's a lot of parts that became rushed as the jam ended, and unfortunately my new and (at the time) favorite optimization technique became a huge hindrance and created harsh biome borders that I couldn't undo within the time frame.

    So while I meant this jam to be a culmination of everything I've learned over the past few months, it instead taught me that there's always more to learn to create an entire world of biomes! This being an end goal of mine with the Jungle being the start!

    If you want to learn how I created this world, I'll be making an interactive document on it at some point!

Experiments and POI


    My portfolio wouldn't be complete if I didn't have experiments! My main experiments have been with graphs or web-like systems. I originally created a mod to do these experiments to allow techniques that would allow procedurally generated branches on trees, however, a few weeks later Hytale ended up adding its own Graphs system which allows this and more including experimental dungeon tech before official dungeon tech was added!

    But anyways, I'm getting ahead of myself. I also found a technique to create erosion on mountains, stop-motion world gen, and have started work on a biome chance modifier for Minecraft! All of this while taking art breaks to create many different procedurally generated Points of Interest (POI) which has definitely become one of my favorite parts of World Gen.

    If you want to talk more in depth about any of this stuff, I'd be over the moon! I didn't want to add a 10 page essay here of my ramblings and excitement for this amazing tech. So with that, I'll just say here's to many more years of World Gen tech! 🎉
