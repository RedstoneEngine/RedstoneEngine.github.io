World Generation


    World Generation has become a long and fun journey of mine these past few months. From learning everything I can about Minecraft's World Generation to learning how to use the recent game Hytale's world generation tools to then providing many guides and creating many of my own worlds utilizing all that I have learned!

    What started out as a hobby has became so much more and has easily become one of my top job prospects combining my love of math with my joy of creating art, experiences, and ecosystems! I hope to implement this knowledge into my Unity and Unreal projects designing more and more game pieces that blend into it such as the player interactions, the story, and creatures that reside in it. I have listed below my many different guides, worlds, and pieces of art, but as I have done a lot on Twitter and Discord, I'm always open to answer any questions and help out with others world building projects!
