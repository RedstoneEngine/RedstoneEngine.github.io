Wormhole Ascent


    Wormhole Ascent is a multiplayer, race to the top minigame mod for the recent game Hytale! Traverse through a wormhole of 4 procedurally generated layers of different biomes requiring different techniques for traversal. Inspired by the Minecraft modpack, Abyssal Ascent, this mod gave me an excuse to use my recently learned procedural world generation knowledge towards a 3 month long Hytale modding contest!

    Creating and designing worlds with the player and balancing in mind became quite a challenge as there's lots of different progressions and different enemies Hytale contains. In the end, I ended up with The Underside being a precarious, devoid, chaotic beginning level leading into the most dangerous level, Ancient Log, containing ferocious dinosaurs you must constantly find ways to sneak and run away from. This leads into the Winter Twist, a battle arena with hidden peaceful sanctuaries of loot. Finally leading to the Volcano which combines all the generation and techniques of previous levels!
